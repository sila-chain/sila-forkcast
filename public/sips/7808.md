## Abstract

This SIP reserves a [transaction-type](./sip-2718.md) range for use by the Rollup Improvement Proposal (RIP) process to ensure there are no conflicts.

## Motivation

For L2s to use new transactrion types, it is necessary to reserve a transaction-type range for use by the RIP process so as to ensure there are no conflicts between transaction types used by RIPs and SIPs.

## Specification

The transaction-type (as specified in [SIP-2718](./sip-2718)) range from `0x40` to `0x7f` (inclusive of both) is reserved for use by the RIP process.

## Rationale

By reserving a transaction-type range for RIPs, it allows the RIP process to maintain its own registry of transaction types that are not (necessarily) in use on L1 sila-mainnet, the SIP process is then freed from having to maintain a registry of RIP tx-types while still having 64 tx-types for its own use.
