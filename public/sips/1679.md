## Abstract

This meta-SIP specifies the changes included in the Sila hardfork named Istanbul.

## Specification

- Codename: Istanbul

### Activation
  - `Block >= 9,069,000` on the Sila SilaMainnet
  - `Block >= 6,485,846` on the Ropsten testnet
  - `Block >= 14,111,141` on the SilaKovan testnet
  - `Block >= 5,435,345` on the Rinkeby testnet
  - `Block >= 1,561,651` on the Görli testnet

### Included SIPs
  - [SIP-152](./sip-152.md): Add Blake2 compression function `F` precompile
  - [SIP-1108](./sip-1108.md): Reduce alt_bn128 precompile gas costs
  - [SIP-1344](./sip-1344.md): Add ChainID opcode
  - [SIP-1884](./sip-1884.md): Repricing for trie-size-dependent opcodes
  - [SIP-2028](./sip-2028.md): Calldata gas cost reduction
  - [SIP-2200](./sip-2200.md): Rebalance net-metered SSTORE gas cost with consideration of SLOAD gas cost change
